Registration links, one per school

We create your school's household registration link and your private review link. Each registration link has an end date, we can see how many households have used it, and we can switch off a registration link the moment it's misused.

One link works in English and Spanish.

Only confirmed households come in

A registration reaches our team only after you confirm it. Staff then bring it into the drive with one click.

If the same household looks registered at two schools, the app flags it and we pause that child until it's sorted out, so no child ends up with two sponsors or none.

Sponsors, matched by our staff

Church members sign up to sponsor through OneLife's Church Center. Our staff assign each child to a sponsor, who receives a gift card with the child's first name, grade, sizes, wish list, and any specific needs, aiming for about $50 per child.

Gifts tracked to your door

We track every gift from the sponsor to our team, fill any gaps from a shopping list, and deliver each household's gifts to you at the school. High schoolers (grades 9 to 12) aren't sponsored; they're on their own list for a shop day.

What keeps this safe depends on two links

Household registration link: send it only to the households you're referring. Every registration through it lands on your school's list, so a link posted publicly lets strangers register under your school.

Your counselor review link: never share it with anyone, including families and coworkers. It opens your school's full list of registered children. If it's ever shared by mistake, tell us right away.

Access rules are enforced by the system itself, not just hidden on screen, and every change our team makes is recorded in an audit log. Families never need to give an address, and children are listed by first name only.
