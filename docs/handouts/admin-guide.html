Admin procedures: running the season

As an admin you can do everything a volunteer can, plus the steps that set up the season or touch something sensitive. This guide lists those steps as short procedures in the order the season runs. Read the volunteer guide first for the basics, and the area guides (Intake, Sponsors, Gifts) for day-to-day work.

Your database contact

Some jobs can't be done inside the app. They go to your database contact, the person who manages the app behind the scenes. Write their name and phone at the end of this guide before the season starts, and allow a day or two for requests. They handle: creating logins, resetting forgotten passwords, removing a person, deleting a household, child, or sponsor, fixing a school saved with a typo, and the drop-off details printed in sponsor emails.

Only admins can

  • Create a drive and change its status
  • Add a school
  • Change roles and confidential access
  • Read the audit log
  • Import registrations
  • Create, cancel, and email school links
  • Put a child on hold and release it
  • Change a saved wish list

Can't be undone in the app

  • A school's name, code, or counselor email once saved
  • Whether a household is confidential
  • Revoking a household registration link
  • Moving a gift forward on Fulfillment
  • Importing the sponsor list twice (it duplicates everyone)

The database contact can fix any of these. Also note: drive status, role, and access changes save the moment you pick them, with no confirm step.

Confidential households

A confidential household is hidden from volunteers unless you tick their "Confidential households" box on Staff & permissions. The one exception is a volunteer who typed it in on Add a household; they still see the ones they entered. Approved volunteers see children as an initial ("J.") in lists. Admins see everything. Every volunteer can see every household that isn't confidential.

  • Choose it when you import or add the household. It can't be switched in the app afterward, and until it is, every volunteer can read it.
  • The original registration stays visible. Ticking confidential at import hides the household, but the registration it came from can still be read by every volunteer on Intake → Registrations, with names and phone. If that matters, ask the database contact.
  • Sponsors and gifts are handled by hand. The app can't assign a sponsor to a confidential child, so their cards stay in Unassigned. Keep that list out of email and shared spreadsheets.

A1 · Open this year's drive

Once, before anything else
Setup → Drives. Everything in the app belongs to the one drive that's Open.
  1. First season in the app?

    If a 2026 drive is already Open, use it and skip to step 3. If the app only shows "No drive with status 'open' was found", ask the database contact to open one.

  2. Create it.

    Click Create drive. Year and name fill in as next year, so change them to 2026 and "Reach Christmas 2026". Pick "School drop-off" as the default distribution and click Create drive. It starts as Draft.

  3. Set the registration window.

    Click Edit and fill in "Referral window start" and "Referral window end" (the dates households can register). The end date becomes the default expiry for new registration links.

  4. Switch drives in one visit.

    If last year's drive is Open, set its Status to Closed, then set the new one to Open, without leaving the page. Only one drive can be open. Then reload any other tabs you have on the app.

Never leave the app with no Open drive. If you reload with none open, every staff page, Drives included, shows "No drive with status 'open' was found" and only the database contact can fix it.

A2 · Give someone access

A few days before their first shift
The database contact creates the login; you set access in Setup → Staff & permissions.
  1. Ask the database contact for the login.

    Give them the person's name and email. They set a starting password. No invite email goes out.

  2. Set their access.

    They appear on Staff & permissions as Volunteer with no confidential access. To make someone an admin, change Role to Admin. To let a volunteer see confidential households, tick Confidential households. Give that to as few people as possible.

  3. Hand over the login privately.

    In person or by phone, not in a group chat. Give them the volunteer guide and ask them to change their password.

  4. Confirm it worked.

    Have them sign in while you're there and check they reach the Dashboard.

You can't change your own row, so you can't lock yourself out. Keep at least two admins so one can fix the other's access.

A3 · Add schools and send counselor links

Before registration opens
Setup → Schools, then the Registration links panel at the bottom of Intake → Registrations.
  1. Add the school.

    Add school: the name; a card code of 1 to 3 letters (like "OL") that starts every household tag and card number; the counselor's name and email as liaison; and how gifts reach households (School drop-off, Church pickup, or the drive's default). Double-check before saving. Schools can't be edited in the app.

  2. Make the household registration link.

    In Registration links, open the school and click New link. "Expires" is required and defaults to the end of the registration window. Click Create link.

  3. Make the counselor review link.

    In the same school, check the counselor's email and click Create counselor link. This is the counselor's private page for confirming households.

  4. Send them in two separate messages.

    First, the household registration link (Copy full link) for the counselor to pass to households. Second, the counselor review link (Copy link) for the counselor only, with the two counselor handouts from the i menu. Once email is turned on, the Email buttons send these to the liaison email for you.

The review link works like a password: it shows every child registered at that school. Never put it in a message that might be forwarded to a household.

A4 · Import confirmed registrations

A few times a week
Dashboard tile "Ready to import", or Intake → Registrations. Full steps are in the Intake guide.
  1. Open each one under "Confirmed by counselor, ready to import".

    Read the Counselor corrections box (applied at import). Check the list row and the page for a "Possibly the same household" note.

  2. Import.

    Import this household → tick "Create this as a confidential household" if the counselor or household has raised a safety or privacy concern → Import now. Children in grades 9 to 12 go on the High schoolers list automatically.

  3. Nudge slow counselors.

    "Waiting on counselor" registrations can't be imported, and there's no override. The Waiting group has a Copy button for each school's review link so you can resend it.

A5 · Sort out a possible double registration

When you see a match note
Intake → Households (open the household), then Intake → On hold.
  1. Decide if it's really a double.

    "Possibly the same household (same guardian phone)" often means a household with children at two schools. That's two households on purpose and fine. It's a problem only when the same child is in twice.

  2. Put the extra child on hold.

    Open the household, click Put this child on hold under the child, give a reason, and click Put on hold. If that card already has a sponsor, cancel it on Fulfillment first.

  3. Check with the counselor, then decide.

    Not a double: Release hold on Intake → On hold. A real double: leave it on hold (it's out of every count) and ask the database contact if it must be removed.

A6 · Load sponsors and assign children

After Church Center sign-ups close, then as needed
Sponsors → Sponsor roster, then Sponsors → Assign sponsors. Full steps are in the Sponsors guide.
  1. Import the roster once.

    CSV with columns name, email, phone, capacity → Import CSV → Preview import → Import 42. Add late sign-ups with + Add sponsor.

  2. Assign.

    Pick a sponsor, then click Assign, or Assign all 3 for a whole household. Or use Suggest matches…, review the draft, and save it. If you go over a sponsor's capacity (how many they offered), the app warns but still lets you.

  3. Match confidential children by hand.

    They aren't listed on Assign sponsors. Keep that list on paper, stored safely, and shred it after hand-off.

A7 · Get cards to sponsors

The same week you assign
Sponsors → Print cards, or Sponsors → Sponsor roster.
  1. While email is off: print.

    Print this page, then Mark printed on each card. The list includes children with no sponsor yet, so match paper to sponsors by card number.

  2. Once email is on: send.

    First ask the database contact to confirm the drop-off place, days, and deadline that print in every email. Then Email all assigned → Send emails. Later, Remind all assigned re-emails sponsors with gifts not yet in. It only reaches sponsors the app emailed, so contact paper-card sponsors yourself.

The 10-day "late" clock starts when a child is assigned, not when the card reaches the sponsor.

A8 · Chase late gifts and hand off to schools

From the first drop-off day
Dashboard "Sponsors running late", Gifts → Fulfillment, Gifts → Distribution. Full steps are in the Gifts guide.
  1. Follow up on late sponsors.

    The Dashboard lists sponsors 10 or more days past assignment, with contact details. Critically overdue is 21 or more days.

  2. Replace a sponsor who drops out.

    On Fulfillment, click Cancel on each of their cards so the children return to Assign sponsors. Then mark the sponsor inactive on the roster.

  3. Hand off.

    Mark each card delivered on Fulfillment, and each household handed to its counselor on Distribution. One doesn't update the other.

Fulfillment has no step back. If a volunteer marks the wrong card, the database contact corrects it. Cancel is only for removing a sponsor.

A9 · A link was shared by mistake

Right away
Intake → Registrations → Registration links → the school.
  1. Counselor review link shared:

    Replace this link… → tick "I understand the current link will stop working right away." → Kill old link and create new one. Send the counselor the new link privately.

  2. Household link posted publicly:

    Revoke it (this can't be undone), make a New link, and send it to the counselor. Strangers' registrations can't be imported unless the counselor confirms them.

A10 · Account problems

As they come up
  • Forgot password: there's no reset link. The database contact sets a new one; the person changes it under Account → Change password.
  • Someone leaves, or a login may be compromised: right away, set their Role to Volunteer and untick Confidential households. Then call the database contact the same day: until the login is removed it can still see every household that isn't confidential.

A11 · Find out who changed something

When a record looks wrong

Setup → Audit log. Pick a table (Households, Children, Cards, Assignments...) and click an entry's summary to see what changed. It shows the latest 200 changes. Sponsor roster edits only show under "All tables". It holds real personal details, so don't copy it out of the app.

A12 · End of season: leave the drive open

After every household is handed off

Leave this year's drive Open until next year's is ready. Closing it with nothing else open locks every staff page. When next season starts, follow A1: create the new drive, then close the old one and open the new one in the same visit.
