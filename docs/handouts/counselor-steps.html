Your steps as a school counselor

You'll get two links from OneLife Church. They look alike, but they follow opposite rules. Please read this box before you send anything.

Share with households

Household registration link

Send it only to households you're referring.

Every registration made through it is tagged to your school and comes to you to confirm.

  • Email or text it directly to each household you're referring.
  • Don't post it on social media, a school website, or a public flyer. Anyone holding it can register under your school.
  • It stops working when registration closes.
Private: never share

Your counselor review link

For you only. Not families, not coworkers, not anyone.

It opens the list of every child registered at your school. Treat it like a password.

  • Never forward the email it came in, or paste it into a group chat or shared document.
  • Never send it to a family by mistake in place of the registration link.
  • Bookmark it for yourself. Shared by accident, or changing roles? Contact us right away.

Step by step

  1. Send the registration link to the households you're referring.

    One form per household, even if the children go to different schools. It takes about five minutes, with an English / Español button at the top. We only ask for children's first names, and never an address.

  2. Households fill it out.

    When they submit, they're told their registration is with you now and that you'll confirm it with us.

  3. Open your review link.

    New registrations show up under Waiting on you. Please check it a couple of times a week while registration is open.

  4. Confirm or flag each household.

    You're confirming that it's a real household you referred and that the children listed look right. Nothing more. Fix a typo in a child's first name, age, or grade if you need to, then click Confirm this household, type your name, and click Confirm. Something off? Click This isn't right and add a short reason. If they registered twice, tick the duplicate box.

  5. Closer to Christmas: gifts come to you.

    OneLife Church delivers each household's gifts to you at the school, and you arrange with each family how they receive them. High schoolers (grades 9 to 12) shop for themselves on a shop day; you'll pass those details on. For a household without email, we'll send updates through you.

What your review page shows: each child's first name, age, and grade. Wish lists, sizes, and household contact details are left off on purpose. Sponsors never see a last name, address, school, or contact information.
