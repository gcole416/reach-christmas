Sponsors: the roster, matching, and cards

Church members sign up to sponsor on Church Center (the church's online sign-up), not in this app. Our job is to load that list, match each child to a sponsor, and get each sponsor their child's card. It all lives under Sponsors in the menu. New to the app? Read the volunteer guide first.

The three Sponsors pages

PageWhat it's for
Sponsor rosterEveryone who signed up this year: contact details, capacity (how many children they offered to buy for), how many they have, notes, and whether their cards were emailed. Shows Active sponsors unless you change the Status filter.
Assign sponsorsSponsors on the left, children waiting on the right. Pick a sponsor, then click children. Brothers and sisters are grouped so a household can go to one sponsor.
Print cardsThe paper card for each child: card number, first name, grade, sizes, wish list, and needs. Mark each one printed once it's off the printer.

Volunteers and admins can do everything on these pages. Removing a sponsor completely is done by an admin.

Who you won't see on Assign sponsors

High schoolers (they shop for themselves), children on hold, and confidential households. The app can't assign confidential children yet, so admins match those outside the app. That's why the Dashboard's "Need a sponsor" number can be higher than the list you see.

What a sponsor may know

A child's first name, grade, sizes, wish list, specific needs, and card number. That's all that's on the card. If a sponsor calls or emails, never share a child's last name, school, or anything about the household, and don't explain what the letters in a card number mean. Point them to the church drop-off details instead.

Common tasks

Assign children to a sponsor

  1. Pick a sponsor on the left.

    Sponsors with the most room are at the top. If staff added a note (for example "two girls" or "same household as last year"), it shows under their name.

  2. Click children on the right.

    Click Assign for one child, or Assign all 3 to keep a household together. Each child's line shows grade, age, and sex, and you can filter by school, sex, and grade.

  3. Made a mistake?

    Click Undo in the message at the bottom. It's there for 10 seconds. After that, don't try to fix it by reassigning. Tell your lead the card number; the Gifts team cancels it on Fulfillment, and your lead tells the sponsor if their card already went out.

Let the app suggest matches

Suggest matches… drafts matches for as many waiting children as sponsors have room for, keeping households together where it can. Nothing saves until you review it. A sponsor with no capacity set is given room for 1. Check sponsors marked "Check the note", click × to take out anything that's wrong, then click Save 40 assignments (the number is how many are in the draft). Children the draft couldn't place are listed under "Not placed by the draft". If someone else assigned a child meanwhile, that one fails and the rest still save.

Get cards to sponsors

Printing: Sponsors → Print cards lists every card not printed yet, including children who don't have a sponsor yet, so it's simplest to print once most children are assigned. Click Print this page, then Mark printed on each card that came out. Tick "Show already-printed cards too" to reprint one.

Email: Email all assigned on the roster sends each sponsor one email with a card for each of their children. Cards already emailed aren't sent again. Remind all assigned re-emails sponsors whose emailed cards went out at least 20 hours ago and whose gifts haven't arrived. Sponsors who got paper cards aren't reminded, so call or email them yourself. Use these only when your lead says email is turned on. Until then the send step shows an error, and cards go out on paper.

A sponsor drops out

First ask the Gifts team to Cancel each of their cards on Fulfillment, so those children come back to Assign sponsors. Then click Mark inactive on the roster. Marking inactive alone leaves their children assigned to them.

The gift clock starts at assignment. A sponsor shows as late 10 days after they're assigned, not 10 days after they get the card. Get cards out the same week you assign. Late sponsors appear on the Dashboard under "Sponsors running late", with phone and email so someone can follow up kindly.

For your lead

Load the Church Center list (once a season)

  1. Shape the export.

    Save Church Center's export as a CSV file with a header row of name, email, phone, capacity. Only name is required. Example row: Jane Smith,jane@example.com,555-0100,2

  2. Preview it.

    Sponsors → Sponsor roster → Import CSV. Choose the file (or paste it) and click Preview import. Nothing is saved yet. Rows with no name are skipped.

  3. Import.

    Click Import 42 (the number is how many rows have a name). Every row lands, or none do.

Import only once. The app doesn't check for repeats, so importing the same file twice adds everyone twice. For late sign-ups use + Add sponsor. Notes don't come in with the import; add them with Edit.
